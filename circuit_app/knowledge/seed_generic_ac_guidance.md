# Generic AC guidance seed — NOT a repair manual

`EVIDENCE_TYPE: GENERIC; SCOPE: AC consumer prechecks; NO exact model, SKU or brand-specific implied compatibility.`

General non-invasive safe questions can include whether the AC is switched to cooling mode, whether the desired temperature is below room temperature, whether displayed error codes are visible without removing covers, and whether indoor airflow seems reduced. Such observations alone do not prove a bad compressor, PCB, capacitor, refrigerant leak or other component. If there is burning smell, exposed wiring, or unsafe condition: stop and escalate. Do not coach household to open panels, handle live electricity or refrigerant.
